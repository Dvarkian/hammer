Hammer

Icon concepts — actual hammers

Eight more. Every one is a real tool, drawn from a specific hammer family. No abstraction, no metaphor. Pick the shape and the object itself.

Standard Claw Hammer
The classic. Rectangular head, curved claw hidden at the end, hickory handle with grip rings and a wedge at the tail. Everything you'd expect from a real claw hammer.
Ball-Peen Hammer
Rounded peen at one end, flat face at the other, checkered handle with brass wedge accents. The ball peen shape shows in the rounded end.
Rubber Mallet
Cylindrical rubber head, dark red, with a thick wood handle. Roundover and flat faces show the mallet shape rather than a claw hammer wedge and claw.
Modern Claw Hammer
Black vulcanized grip with raised ridges, vibrantly colored accent ring near the base, steel head with a slight chamfer at the back. A contemporary take on the claw hammer.
Titanium & Blue
A titanium/silver head paired with a blue Denali-style handle. Tapered handle profile, blue gradient with subtle grip texture and accent dots. Industrial but with a distinctive color accent.
Line-Art Claw
Simplified silhouette: bold head shape, visible claw at the end, clean wood handle with grain line and wedge. Less rendering, more graphic shape — but still clearly a claw hammer.
Masonry / Chipping Hammer
A blocky masonry hammer with a chisel edge on one side and a flat face on the other. Short, thick handle with a wider grip section. Angular, brutalist — nothing curved or subtle about it.
Upholsterer's Hammer
Small, compact head with a long, thin handle. The rounded head and extended grip make it read as a different tool from the standard claw hammer — a specialist's instrument.
