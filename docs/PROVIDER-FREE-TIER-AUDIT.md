# Provider free-tier audit — does it recur?

**Audit date:** 2026-09-25 (second pass; see [Corrections](#g-corrections-this-second-pass-made-to-the-first))
· **Audited:** 71 providers (17 hammer-owned, 54 imported) · **Registry now:** 47 providers
(16 hammer-owned, 31 imported) · **Removed:** `longcat`, then the whole of bucket C (2026-09-25)

## The question, and what counts as an answer

Hammer routes _free_ inference, so the distinction that matters is not "is there a free tier"
but **"does the free access come back?"** One question, applied to every registered provider:

> **Recurring free usage** = free access that renews on a clock — per request window, per
> day, per week, per month — without a purchase, a top-up or a renewal, and that the vendor
> actually publishes.

Explicitly **not** recurring free usage: a one-time signup credit or trial; a
deposit-unlocked quota; a promo, preview or public beta with an announced end; and anything
whose only source is a third party guessing.

**Evidence standard.** Every row names its source and the date the source speaks to. Vendor
documentation outranks third-party lists; where the two disagree, both are recorded and the
row is marked **contested**. Anything the vendor does not publish is marked **unresolved**
rather than guessed. The third-party sources relied on here, with their dates:

- **[ARef]** OmniRoute `PROVIDER_REFERENCE.md` v3.8.50, generated 2026-08-21 — one-line
  free-tier statement per provider, generated from OmniRoute's own provider constants.
- **[AW]** `mnfst/awesome-free-llm-apis` — curated list whose inclusion rule is explicitly
  "trial credits and time-limited promos don't count"; per-model rate limits with citations.
- **[KY]** klymentiev.com "Free LLM API 2026: 17 Tested", last updated 2026-09-11, limits
  "checked against the provider's own docs that day".
- **[IN]** In-tree live probe evidence (HTTP probes recorded in this repository, 2026-09-22 /
  2026-09-23) or curated notes in `lib/providers/`.

## F. What the tail means for routing

- **The tail of this audit has been acted on in full.** Every row it named was in bucket C, and
  all 24 are now excluded from the roster: the two dead services (`predibase`, `monsterapi`),
  the misclassified `stepfun`, the four refusals that were refusals because nothing recurs
  (`cerebras`, `deepseek`, `deepinfra`, `freemodel-dev`), the duplicate Kilo gateway, and the two
  client-gated Zen rows whose free tier exists only inside OpenCode's own client.
- **Three staged rows do have recurring free access** (`huggingchat`, `t3-web`, `agy`) and are
